# Company Overview & Practice

## Mission & Identity
We are a specialized AI Automation and Product Engineering consultancy. We partner with ambitious technology companies, SaaS scale-ups, and modern operational teams to design, build, and deploy production-grade AI systems that create real commercial leverage.

We are not an agency that wraps simple chat prompts; we are software engineers who build robust, observable, and secure AI workflows integrated deeply into existing enterprise infrastructure.

## Core Capabilities
- **Real-Time Voice AI**: Sub-second latency digital receptionists, appointment coordinators, and voice assistants powered by Gemini Live and WebRTC/WebSockets.
- **Enterprise RAG Systems**: Context-aware retrieval-augmented generation over internal documentation, code repositories, CRM histories, and knowledge bases.
- **Inbound Sales Automation**: AI qualification engines that capture intent, calculate lead qualification, and schedule discovery calls directly into CRM systems.
- **Customer Support Engineering**: Tier-1 and Tier-2 AI support agents that resolve tickets, invoke business APIs, and execute refunds or lookups with safety guardrails.
- **Custom Agent Engineering**: Stateful multi-step agents that execute complex business processes with humans in the loop.

## Engagement Model
We operate in focused, sprint-based cycles:
1. **Technical Discovery & Feasibility (Week 1)**: Architecture review, data audit, and tool requirements definition.
2. **Prototype & Sandbox Validation (Weeks 2–3)**: Working system in a sandboxed staging environment with deterministic evaluation.
3. **Production Deployment & Telemetry (Weeks 3–5)**: Live roll-out with latency tracking, fallbacks, and CRM integration.
4. **Iterative Optimization**: Continuous evaluation based on real conversation logs and tool success metrics.
