# Services & Solutions Catalog

## Overview of Offerings
All services provided by our team are engineered to integrate cleanly into your existing stack (Next.js, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Supabase, HubSpot, Salesforce, Google Calendar, Zendesk, Slack).

### 1. AI Voice Agents & Digital Receptionists
- **Target Audience**: Clinics, legal/financial firms, SaaS sales teams, and customer support desks receiving inbound calls.
- **Key Features**: Bidirectional streaming audio, tool calling for appointment scheduling, caller identification, CRM data updates, and graceful warm transfers to human staff.
- **Demo Pricing**: $4,500 initial setup + $350/month maintenance & support (Includes custom voice prompt, tool schemas, and telephony/web bridge).
- **Delivery Timeline**: 2–4 weeks from kick-off to deployment.

### 2. AI Customer Support Systems
- **Target Audience**: E-commerce, B2B SaaS, and logistics platforms handling high repetitive ticket volumes.
- **Key Features**: Direct integration with Zendesk/Intercom/Freshdesk, autonomous order tracking, return authorization, knowledge base search, and automated ticket summary logging.
- **Demo Pricing**: $3,800 initial setup + $290/month maintenance (Covers up to 5 integrations and SLA monitoring).
- **Delivery Timeline**: 2–3 weeks.

### 3. Enterprise Knowledge Assistants (RAG)
- **Target Audience**: Engineering teams, legal counsel, HR, and operations departments managing fragmented documentation.

- **Delivery Timeline**: 3–5 weeks.

### 4. AI Inbound Sales Qualification Assistant
- **Target Audience**: Consultancies, agencies, and high-ticket B2B sales pipelines.
- **Key Features**: Captures prospect intent, calculates deterministic qualification score (0–100), offers real-time calendar slots, and writes lead records directly into CRM.
- **Demo Pricing**: $3,500 initial setup + $250/month maintenance.
- **Delivery Timeline**: 2–3 weeks.

### 5. Custom AI Agent & Workflow Engineering

- **Key Features**: Custom tools, persistent state machines, human approval queues, sandbox execution, and structured observability.
- **Demo Pricing**: $8,000 – $20,000 per engagement depending on complexity and number of integrated systems.
